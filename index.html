Overview

Data pulled live from the source spreadsheet on each visit; the last good copy is kept in your browser so the page still works offline. Handicaps use the Archery GB 2023 scheme: lower is better, so handicap axes are inverted and an upward line means improvement. Classification scores come from archeryutils and may occasionally differ by a point from the official tables. See also the Duston Bowmen club records and the club score tracker for any member's sheet.
